1. PROJECT CONTEXT
AquaInSAR is a Conrad Challenge concept (team The Oopsies, Aerospace & Aviation) studying
whether Sentinel-1-derived surface deformation can be useful evidence about groundwater
stress in an agricultural area of Uttar Pradesh. The repository explicitly treats the
"predict collapse before wells run dry" phrase as an aspiration, not a capability, and
requires deformation observations to be kept separate from groundwater interpretation
(docs/research-and-validation.md; docs/decisions/0001-evidence-first-claims.md).
No pilot district, real dataset, scene manifest, deformation product, model, or dashboard
exists in the checkout. Four candidate areas were screened by MF but not scored or selected.

2. ASSIGNED RESPONSIBILITIES

data contract (human-readable and machine-readable), an exploratory comparison protocol,
supporting implementation where justified, tests, a QA report, a README, this log, and a
project-context map. W2–W7 tasks are explicitly out of scope.
