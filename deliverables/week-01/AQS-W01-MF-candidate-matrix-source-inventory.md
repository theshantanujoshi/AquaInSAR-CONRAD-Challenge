## Purpose and claim boundary

This deliverable compares at least three plausible Uttar Pradesh candidate areas and records the groundwater, rainfall, aquifer/geological, monitoring-well, radar, and validation sources located for each. It is a screening inventory, not a scored selection and not a validation result.

**Target region TBD; placeholders used until selection by project lead.** The final district/block boundary is `[REGION] to be selected` by SJ under AQ-01. Source availability does not prove groundwater stress; an extraction-stage category is not a measured water-level trend; and deformation is not evidence of aquifer depletion until it is compared with suitable ground records and alternative explanations are checked.

Candidate areas are screening units only. Proposed boundaries will be converted to versioned geometries after SJ's decision.

### Selection-method criteria

The criteria were derived from the cited methods, not from data convenience:

- **Documented stress:** prefer spatially resolved, authoritative extraction-stage/category and water-level evidence. CGWB block categories are used as screening evidence only; a category is not a trend. [S1], [S20]
- **Reference-data coverage:** check well density, record length, sampling frequency, coordinates/CRS, datum, screen/aquifer, missingness, and rainfall overlap. IGRAC guidance requires network design to match spatial variability and hydrogeology. [S3], [S20]
- **InSAR feasibility:** require a consistent Sentinel-1 track, sufficient acquisitions, manageable baselines/geometry, and a coherence test. USGS guidance flags atmosphere, vegetation, standing water, tilling, temporal separation, DEM/topographic effects, and over-smoothing as sources of error or decorrelation. [S10], [S11], [S19]
- **Hydrogeologic plausibility:** prefer alluvial, compressible, confined/semi-confined systems with a testable groundwater mechanism; treat hard-rock zones as contrast candidates unless local deformation evidence exists. [S8], [S19]
- **Validation path:** require a realistic route to independent ground records, expert review, or partner access before any validation claim. [S3], [S6], [S12], [S14], [S20]
- **Confounder control:** keep urban NCR and Lucknow deformation studies as precedent, not as proof for a rural agricultural pilot; check construction, transport infrastructure, tectonic signals, seasonal effects, and processing artefacts. [S12], [S13], [S14], [S19]

2. `[REGION] to be selected` denotes the final pilot boundary. It must be replaced by a versioned geometry and named districts/blocks after the decision.
3. For any block where a direct public well series cannot be confirmed, record: `No publicly available groundwater level data in [region] – may require contacting local agencies.` This is an access placeholder, not a claim about all Uttar Pradesh.
4. Where `U` appears, the item is unknown or inaccessible; it must not be scored as zero.
5. Stage of groundwater extraction above 100% in a CGWB block indicates extraction exceeding the estimated annual extractable resource under that assessment method. It is not, by itself, a measured groundwater-level trend, aquifer compaction measurement, or InSAR validation label.
6. Rainfall values summarized above are 2023 district values from [S3]/[S7]; they are screening context, not a matched seasonal covariate.
7. NAQUIM reports supply maps and conceptual aquifer information at report vintages from 2017–2024; current conditions and vector/CRS details remain unverified.
8. Sentinel-1 entries describe repository-independent archive access. No scene manifest, acquisition count, coherence map, or deformation output has been generated in this repository.
9. No partner has been contacted, no data-sharing agreement exists, and no independent ground reference has been secured.
10. “Spring flow” is `N/A` only for the current alluvial/hard-rock agricultural candidate set; if a spring-fed or karst candidate is added, this column must be replaced with a source inventory.
