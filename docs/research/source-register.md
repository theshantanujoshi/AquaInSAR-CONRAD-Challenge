## Access notes and unresolved issues

- **Full texts.** The four literature entries were checked at abstract/metadata level. Paywalls and rate limits prevented full-text review. Do not present method details beyond the abstract as verified.
- **India-WRIS.** The portal did not respond on 2026-11-26. Station-level access, export format, provenance, and terms remain unverified.
- **CGWB national 2024 report.** A previous attempt to extract page 64 of the national PDF failed with a `pypdf` `LimitReachedError`. The state summary and UP yearbook were used instead. Retry with a different extractor before citing national page-level details.
- **IMD.** District rainfall statistics are cited for context; the page was not re-fetched in this pass and its terms were not reviewed.
- **NISAR.** Availability and maturity are operational facts that can change. Record the product version/CRID and release status for any actual download.
- **Competition deadline.** Official sources disagree: January 7, 2027 (Student Guide, Innovation Stage page, calendar) versus January 8, 2027 (Challenge overview). Use January 7 as the working deadline and confirm with Conrad.
- **Category.** The repository records Aerospace & Aviation. The Water Challenge is a plausible alternative. Neither this register nor the dossier changes the registered category.
