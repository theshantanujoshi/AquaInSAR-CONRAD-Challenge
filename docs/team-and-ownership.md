## Role ownership

### SJ — Project Lead / Technical Advisor

**Owns:** project milestones, priorities, and scope; review of scientific assumptions and technical decisions; coordination of the four verticals and dependencies; review of research methodology, experiments, and evidence; Conrad Challenge submissions, pitch strategy, and presentation; connections with researchers, groundwater experts, and potential pilot partners.

**Primary outputs:** project decisions, milestone reviews, integration decisions, claim-evidence reviews, submission readiness, and external engagement coordination.

### MF — Groundwater Research Scientist

**Owns:** the scientific connection between satellite observations and groundwater conditions. Research aquifer depletion and subsidence mechanisms; collect groundwater-level and rainfall datasets; identify CGWB and state records; investigate local geology and aquifer characteristics; compare deformation with groundwater measurements; design validation and field-interview methods; identify conditions where subsidence is not a reliable stress indicator.

**Primary outputs:** groundwater and rainfall reference datasets; pilot-region hydrogeological profile; satellite-to-ground validation report; evidence supporting, challenging, or qualifying project assumptions.

### AC — Machine Learning / Research Engineer

**Owns:** analytical methods that combine deformation, groundwater, rainfall, and seasonal data; relevant geospatial and temporal features; stress indicators; justified statistical or machine-learning approaches; model performance and uncertainty; false-positive/false-negative and lead-time evaluation where data permit.

**Primary outputs:** integrated modelling dataset; reproducible analytical pipeline; justified stress/risk indicator or model; evaluation and uncertainty report; risk maps and classifications where supported.

### SM — Geospatial Research Engineer

**Owns:** satellite data acquisition and ground-deformation analysis. Research Sentinel-1 SAR and interferometry; acquire and preprocess imagery; develop the InSAR workflow; estimate displacement and investigate subsidence; produce deformation maps/time series; document noise, atmospheric effects, and processing limits.

**Primary outputs:** Sentinel-1 processing pipeline; deformation maps for the selected region; displacement time-series dataset; methodology and limitations documentation.

### AP — Product & Venture Development Engineer

**Owns:** making supported research outputs usable and testing the venture assumptions. Design and develop the dashboard; map risk zones; integrate InSAR and analytical outputs; prototype location-specific visualizations and SMS/WhatsApp alerts; conduct user research; develop business-model, pricing, and deployment assumptions; maintain product documentation and demonstrations.

**Primary outputs:** functional dashboard; interactive geospatial visualization; demonstrable alert prototype; customer-discovery report; business model and deployment plan.

## Ownership and task rules

- Each task has one directly accountable owner. Collaborators do not replace that owner.
- Only SJ changes the team's role-ownership mapping.
- A task is not complete based only on a verbal status. Its acceptance criteria must be met and its evidence reviewed.
- Keep task status distinct from milestone completion and validated capability.
- Record task ID, owner, vertical, objective, deliverable, deadline, dependencies, acceptance criteria, status, and evidence.
- Status meanings: **Not Started**, **In Progress**, **Blocked**, **In Review**, and **Completed**.
- Do not invent progress, deadlines, or evidence. The current task register is [tasks/todo.md](../tasks/todo.md); detailed definitions are in [tasks/plan.md](../tasks/plan.md).

## Collaboration boundaries

The data contract connects the four student verticals. Each owner documents input/output fields and quality limitations before integration. SJ coordinates the interfaces and reviews claims. Student owners remain responsible for understanding and defending their own work.
