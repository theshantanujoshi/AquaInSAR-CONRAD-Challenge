# ADR-0001: Separate Deformation Observations from Groundwater-Risk Claims

- Status: Accepted as a project operating principle
- Date: 2026-11-20
- Deciders: SJ as Project Lead; student vertical owners apply it in their deliverables

## Context

AquaInSAR proposes using InSAR-derived ground deformation to investigate groundwater stress. Subsidence is an indirect signal. Geological conditions, compaction, tectonic activity, seasonal movement, atmospheric effects, decorrelation, and measurement noise can influence the observation. The team's master project context requires correlation to be distinguished from causation and prohibits claiming validation without actual validation evidence.

## Decision

We will report deformation first as a satellite-derived observation with its acquisition dates, method, quality indicators, and uncertainty. We will interpret possible groundwater stress only alongside groundwater, rainfall, geological, and seasonal context. Product and competition materials will label exploratory indicators, validation status, uncertainty, and scope. Individual well-failure or groundwater-collapse predictions will not be presented as established capabilities without appropriate independent evidence.

## Consequences

- Research and product outputs must preserve provenance, quality, and uncertainty.
- The team may need to report a negative or inconclusive result if evidence is weak or incompatible.
- The dashboard can communicate regional observations before a risk model is justified, but must not imply certainty or operational warning performance.
- Claims take longer to make because evidence and independent validation must be reviewed.

## Alternatives considered

### Treat subsidence as direct proof of groundwater depletion

Rejected because deformation has multiple possible causes and is not a universal or immediate indicator of aquifer depletion or individual well failure.

### Present predictive risk classifications before reference labels are available

Deferred. A model or warning metric is appropriate only if the team obtains suitable reference data and evaluates error modes and uncertainty.
